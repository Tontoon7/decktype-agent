# Decktype for Antigravity

Decktype turns what you tell your agent into editable presentations. This plugin connects Antigravity to the Decktype remote connector at `https://api.decktype.com/mcp`.

## What it does

- Create a presentation from the facts you supply, with Decktype themes and slide templates.
- Find your private presentations, read their slides and revise their content, keeping your manual edits.
- Preview a slide and open the presentation in the Decktype editor.

## Getting started

1. Install the plugin, then start a conversation that asks for a presentation.
2. Antigravity asks you to connect your Decktype account the first time a Decktype tool runs. Sign in or create an account at decktype.com and approve the access.
3. Your presentations are saved in Decktype Cloud. Open any of them in the editor from the link your agent returns.

## Data and privacy

The connector only reads and writes the presentations of the Decktype account you connect. Privacy policy: https://decktype.com/en/privacy. Terms: https://decktype.com/en/terms. Documentation: https://decktype.com/en/connectors. Support: https://decktype.com/en/contact.
